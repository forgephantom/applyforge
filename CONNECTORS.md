# Connectors — Google Drive and Gmail

ApplyForge files everything to your Google Drive and verifies submissions
against your Gmail. Both connect from inside the Muse app — nothing is
typed into this repo.

## Google Drive (required)

1. Open the Muse app → **Settings → Connectors**.
2. Find **Google Drive** → **Connect**, and sign in with the Google account
   that holds your `job_resumes` folder.
3. During onboarding, tell Muse which Drive folder to use — it becomes
   `OUT_BASE`, and every application packet is filed under it.

Only jobs you actually applied to are saved — anything prepared but not
submitted is removed.

## Gmail (recommended)

1. Open the Muse app → **Settings → Connectors**.
2. Find **Gmail** → **Connect**, and sign in.
3. Muse uses it to verify application confirmations (some land outside the
   Primary tab — Muse checks the others).

## Updating or switching accounts

Go back to **Settings → Connectors** any time to disconnect, reconnect, or
switch the Google account. Then tell Muse — it re-verifies the connection
before the next Drive write.
